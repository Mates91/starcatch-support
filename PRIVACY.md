# Skycatching — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

**The developer of Skycatching collects nothing.** There is no account of ours, no server of
ours, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives anything
about you or what you do in the app.

The one exception is **Game Center**, Apple's own service for leaderboards,
and only if you are signed in to it. Then the app sends a few numbers to
Apple — see *Game Center* below. Your location is never among them.

## What the app reads on your phone

**Your location.** The app asks for your location while you are using it, and
uses it for two things: working out which stars are above your horizon where
you are standing, and pointing the compass at true north instead of magnetic
north. **Your location is never transmitted, stored on any server, or shared
with anyone.** Refuse the permission and the app still works — it falls back
to a fixed position and says so on screen.

**Motion and the compass.** The app reads the gyroscope, accelerometer and
magnetometer to know which way the phone is pointing. These readings are used
in the moment and are not recorded.

**The camera, only if you switch it on.** There is an optional backdrop that
shows a dimmed live camera view behind the computed stars. It is off by
default. **No frame is ever read, analysed, recorded or transmitted** — the
image is drawn on screen and discarded. Turning it off leaves the app
identical in every other way.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your phone, the app sends these to
Apple's Game Center leaderboards, under your Game Center name:

- how many stars, planets and Moons your collection holds;
- how many you have caught tonight;
- your tournament score, together with the squad you fought with (which
  five stars from that round's offer, as numbers) and the squad's trick.

That is everything. **Your location, your notes, the names you gave stars,
your drawn figures and the collection itself are never sent.** The scores are
stored and shown by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy
(<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>); the developer has no access to them
beyond what any player sees on the leaderboard.

If you are not signed in, nothing is sent, and the app works the same. You
can sign out at any time in the phone's Settings → Game Center.

## What the app stores, and where

The stars you have caught, the figures you have drawn and any notes you have
written are kept in a file inside the app's own storage on your phone. They
are included in your device backup if you have one, and they are deleted when
you delete the app. Nobody but you can read them.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and the developer collects nothing from
anyone, of any age. Game Center is Apple's, and its settings for children are
Apple's too (Screen Time can turn it off).

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party code, no software development kits, no
trackers and no advertising networks. The only outside service it ever talks
to is Apple's Game Center, described above, and only when you are signed in. Its star positions come from
published astronomical catalogues, credited in the app under Setup → Credits,
which are data rather than services and are shipped inside the app.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a
change in what is sent is announced in the app's release notes as well as
here. The Game Center section was added in version 2.1.
