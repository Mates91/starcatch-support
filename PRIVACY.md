# Skycatching — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

**Skycatching collects nothing.** There is no account, no server, no analytics
and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because
there is nowhere for it to go: the app makes no network connections at all.

## What the app reads on your phone

**Your location.** The app asks for your location while you are using it, and
uses it for two things: working out which stars are above your horizon where
you are standing, and pointing the compass at true north instead of magnetic
north. **Your location is never transmitted, stored on any server, or shared
with anyone.** Refuse the permission and the app still works — it falls back
to a fixed position and says so on screen.

**Motion and the compass.** The app reads the gyroscope, accelerometer and
magnetometer to know which way the phone is pointing. These readings are used
in the moment and are not recorded.

**The camera, only if you switch it on.** There is an optional backdrop that
shows a dimmed live camera view behind the computed stars. It is off by
default. **No frame is ever read, analysed, recorded or transmitted** — the
image is drawn on screen and discarded. Turning it off leaves the app
identical in every other way.

## What the app stores, and where

The stars you have caught, the figures you have drawn and any notes you have
written are kept in a file inside the app's own storage on your phone. They
are included in your device backup if you have one, and they are deleted when
you delete the app. Nobody but you can read them.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

## Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party code, no software development
kits, no trackers and no advertising networks. Its star positions come from
published astronomical catalogues, credited in the app under Setup → Credits,
which are data rather than services and are shipped inside the app.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the
app collects nothing, the only change that could matter would be one that
started collecting something — and that would be announced in the app's
release notes as well as here.
